PITCHBOOK - FOUNDER MATRIX is an educational and analytical platform designed to help users understand the relationship between universities, entrepreneurs, startups, venture capital and company funding.

Traditional university rankings mainly focus on academic reputation, research, teaching, employability or citations. Founder Matrix looks at a different dimension: the entrepreneurial outcomes associated with educational institutions.

The purpose of Founder Matrix is to make entrepreneurship-related university data easier to understand, compare and explore.

What You Can Explore

The platform presents information relating to undergraduate, graduate and MBA educational backgrounds of founders. Depending on the available dataset, users can examine founder counts, company counts and capital raised by companies associated with founders from particular institutions.

The platform also provides explanatory information about venture capital investors, angel investors, startup funding and the terminology commonly used in entrepreneurial ecosystems.

Why Founder Matrix?

A university can contribute to entrepreneurship in many ways. Its alumni network, academic environment, technology ecosystem, business education, research facilities, incubation programmes and access to investors can all influence entrepreneurial opportunities.

Founder Matrix therefore provides an additional perspective on institutions rather than attempting to replace conventional university rankings.

Our objective is simple: present complex entrepreneurship data in a clear, accessible and visually engaging manner so that students, educators, researchers and anyone interested in universities and startups can explore it more easily.
